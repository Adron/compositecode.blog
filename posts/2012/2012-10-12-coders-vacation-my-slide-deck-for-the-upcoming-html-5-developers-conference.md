*Continuously Kicking Ass*

Web development isn’t always easy. Really, there isn’t a form of application development that is easy, there’s a lot of steps, tools, and other traps to coding. One of the best unifying ways to insure that all this complexity doesn’t kick your ass, is to implement continuous integration & delivery practices. I’m going to cover that in a talk this Monday at the [HTML 5 Developers Conference](http://html5devconf.com/) in San Francisco. As a precursor to the talk, here’s my slide deck… which I’ll admit isn’t the whole kit and kaboodle, because I will haz codez so subscribe and those I’ll be publishing during this Coder’s Vacation of mine.

**NOTE:** Pending on levels of connectivity, I’ll be showing a number of details, examples and a little code on implementing continuous integration for JavaScript, specifically with vows and Node.js elements.
