Here’s some great events coming up the first week of February for [Portland Startup Week](http://portland.startupweek.co/)! Are you planning on attending any of these or others during the week? Let me know of other good events related to Portland Startup Week and I’ll get those posted too.

**Sailing Away From Dependency Hell with Docker & Fig**
[Tuesday Feb 3: 12-1 PST](http://www.eventbrite.com/e/sailing-away-from-dependency-hell-with-docker-engineering-lunch-n-learn-wquick-left-tickets-15383604776)

**Deconstructing Women in Technology: What’s the Data Really Telling Us**
[Tuesday Feb 3: 5-6 PST](http://www.eventbrite.com/e/deconstructing-women-in-technology-what-is-the-data-really-telling-us-tickets-15383831454)

**Portland Startup Week Where are your Wearables Hackfest with Quick Left & Name.com**
[Wednesday Feb 4: Hackfest: 6:00-10](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/pdx-startup-week-where-are-your-wearables-hackfest-tickets-15211086770)

I’ll be attending the [hackfest](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/pdx-startup-week-where-are-your-wearables-hackfest-tickets-15211086770) and hope to team up with anyone that has been hacking IoT or other hardware and wearables to try to put together something new – or even to discuss what we might build in the future. Either way, it should be a great time and I look forward to teaming up with people to build some awesome.

Cheers!
