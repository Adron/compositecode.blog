[![](https://photos.adron.me/Software/Misc-Images/Just-Icons-and-Bits/i-rLzvSVr/0/S/code-fellows-logo-shield-S.png)](https://www.codefellows.org/)In the coming month there are three courses I’ll be teaching here in the grand city of Portland, Oregon. Two of the courses are one day **Unix & Git for Everyone** ([Jan 31](https://www.codefellows.org/calendar#pdx-w1) and [Feb 28](https://www.codefellows.org/calendar#pdx-w2))  courses and the big course is the **Computer Science & Web Development Bootcamp** ([Feb 2 – Feb 27](https://www.codefellows.org/calendar#pdx-b1)). So if you’re interested in ramping up and becoming a software developer sooner than later, check out the courses and more on the [Code Fellows Site](https://www.codefellows.org/), follow the [PDX Code Fellows Twitter](https://twitter.com/codefellowspdx) or [Code Fellows Twitter](https://twitter.com/codefellowsorg), and [like the Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/codefellows.org).

I’ll have more information about teach, training, mentoring and other projects I have on the cooker over the course of today.
