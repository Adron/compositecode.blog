Nope, gotta do it.  I went around and hit Google for some links.  At this point the key links I’ve found are;

- [Dennis van der Stelt’s Entry](http://bloggingabout.net/blogs/dennis/archive/2005/09/01/9214.aspx) – This one is ancient though (2005).- From the comments of Dennis’ Blog I found [VBA Unit](http://sourceforge.net/projects/vbaunit/) and…- …Richard Jones own link to [VBA Unit](http://www.richardjones.info/home/downloads.html).

These later two items seem to cover a lot of ground for VBA Unit Testing of Excel.  There is however a MAJOR problem.  It is unit testing ***inside*** of Excel.  This I don’t really need, what I need is the ability or a framework to create programmatic (from the VB or C# Code) unit tests of work being done in Excel.  So far I’m not sure how this could or should be done.  I am however, coming to some conclusions and will be posting those soon.  Anyone else out there in the blogosphere that has any ideas, let me know (plz).
