I often get into debates when a group or department attempts to implement Agile, then does a half ass job, then complains about how Agile failed.  One of the issues is exactly what Ross Pettit wrote about in his [Agile Journal](http://www.agilejournal.com/) Article, [Mythical Agile Shortcuts](http://www.agilejournal.com/articles/the-agile-manager/mythical-agile-shortcuts.html).

These myths that he outlines are only the tip of the ice berg.  There are dozens of others that can sink, or stifle a project.  So if you're on an Agile Project, learn these, and avoid them like the management anti-patterns they are.
