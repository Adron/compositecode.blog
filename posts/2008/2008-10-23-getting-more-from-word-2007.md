I have been using Word 2007 a lot lately writing up technical and how to documents for various projects I’m working on. In addition to that I’ve started looking at word for creating my blog entries. I did get word hooked up to publish blog entries against my BlogEngine.NET entries with the help of a blog post, “[Publishing to BlogEngine from Word 2007](http://rtur.net/blog/post/2008/06/07/Publishing-to-BlogEngine-from-Word-2007.aspx)” on [RTUR.NET’s Blog](http://rtur.net/blog/). I also found out where the default theme setting for Word 2007 via the “[Setting the default to your liking](http://blogs.msdn.com/microsoft_office_word/archive/2006/12/08/setting-the-default-to-your-liking.aspx)” entry on [The Microsoft Office Word Team’s Blog](http://blogs.msdn.com/microsoft_office_word/default.aspx). The one issue I have that I haven’t found an answer to is how to change the default document that opens when you launch Word 2007.

So if you know how to change that default document I’d be keen to know how.
