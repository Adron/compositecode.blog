…if you are deciding what to attend this year, here’s the top of the list.

Just a few key conferences that will kick ass in technical & academic content. The other great thing about these conferences is that they either have a “[code of ethics](http://oreilly.com/conferences/code-of-conduct.html)” or are reknown for real conference diversity vs. the “hey a bunch of privileged sameness all ended up in this room purely out of meritocracy” nonsense. So you can rest assured that at these conferences you’ll have interesting conversations, be actively involved in things that will expand our personal sphere of the world and in the end leave you dramatically more enriched than those “*corporate warm body vacation conferences*“. This list doesn’t mean I’ll be able to make it to all of them, but each conference I’m in some way intimately involved with and fully support with the “**[Conference Seal of Approval](http://compositecode.com/coding-community/documentation-for/programmer-definitions/conference-seal-of-approval/)**“! So here we go…

[Write the Docs](http://conf.writethedocs.org/) – April 8th and 9th – [@writethedocs](https://twitter.com/writethedocs) – A new movement, to know where we are, where we’ve been and where we’re going. Get here, know how and why to bring knowledge forth through documentation. This is not your grandpa’s documentation.

[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Software/Meetups-N-Conferences/Write-The-Docs/i-wB5Pf46/0/O/WriteTheDocs.png)](http://conf.writethedocs.org/)

[Portland RailsConf 2013](http://www.railsconf.com/) – April 29th-May 2nd – [@railsconf](https://twitter.com/railsconf) – The rails community has grown by leaps and bounds. Regardless of your love or hate for the framework, it’s revolutionized the way web applications are built over the last decade.

[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Software/Meetups-N-Conferences/Rails-Conf-2013/i-FwQF89z/0/O/RailsConf2013.png)](http://www.railsconf.com/)

[RICON East](http://ricon.io/east.html) in New York City – May 13th & 14th – [@basho](https://twitter.com/basho) – (I do work for Basho, but this is on here because RICON last year ROCKED and meets the requirements, come see for yourself!)  🙂

[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Software/Meetups-N-Conferences/RICONEast/i-fHfrMD5/0/O/RICON.png)](http://ricon.io/)

[Node PDX](http://nodepdx.org/) – May 16th & 17th – [@nodepdx](https://twitter.com/nodepdx) – Troy Howard and I are putting this together, we’re working hard to make sure it’s more of what you want, more than it was last year and kicking to the curb the things you don’t want. So come hack some node, JavaScript and enjoy yourselves.

[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Software/Meetups-N-Conferences/Node-PDX-2013/i-mKbjXsb/0/O/NodePDX.png)](http://nodepdx.com)

[Polyglot 2013](http://www.polyglotconf.com/) – May 24th through the 26th – [@polyglotConf](https://twitter.com/PolyglotConf) – Open spaces, with tons of really, truly smart people with no presumptuous marketing and sales bullshit to get in the way. This is about software development, across the realms of frameworks, languages and more.

[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Software/Meetups-N-Conferences/Polyglot-2013/i-HsKWxwH/0/O/Polyglot.png)](http://www.polyglotconf.com/)

[OS Bridge](http://opensourcebridge.org/) – June 18th through the 21st – [@osbridge](https://twitter.com/osbridge) – open source bridges the divide here and new thinking is created. You have to attend to see…

[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Software/Meetups-N-Conferences/OS-Bridge-2013/i-xSjQCRr/0/O/OS-Bridge.png)](http://opensourcebridge.org/)

[OSCON](http://www.oscon.com/oscon2013) – July 22nd through the 26th – [@oscon](https://twitter.com/oscon) – This is the premier open source conference in North America. It’s in Portland. Nuff’ said.

[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Software/Meetups-N-Conferences/OSCON-2013/i-XCFx5gb/0/O/OSCON.png)](http://www.oscon.com/oscon2013)

I’ll be at a few more conferences this year, but these are the key conferences, if you have to pick one to go to, it should be on this list. If you can go to one or two others, pick em’ from this list. Software + Data + Giant Phat Data + NoSQL + Future Thinking + Leaders o’ Thoughts == Top Conference List.

See ya there. Cheers!
