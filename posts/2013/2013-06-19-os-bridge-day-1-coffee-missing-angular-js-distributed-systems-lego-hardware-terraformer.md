**Kicking Impostor Syndrome in the Head**

This talk tackled the ideas of how to be more inclusive, allow people to actually gain buy in and confidence in the work they’re doing. This is a hugely important set of ideas that most of the large corporate world has no clue about. Thus the dramatically lower productivity, individual leadership, pride and happiness that people have working in large corporate enterprises & especially Government. This is a space that should be an extremely high priority for those businesses to study.

[![Mistakes...](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Software/Meetups-N-Conferences/OS-Bridge-2013/i-7srBM9j/0/S/IMG_4200-S.jpg "Mistakes...")](http://www.dreamwidth.org/)

*Mistakes…*

Denise Paolucci did a great job engaging the crowd and relaying the ideas of how to improve work environments to really bring out the best in people. Simply, it occurred to me this could be summarized as, “*Don’t be a dick, how to kick ass, and build the whole team to do just that!*”

The talk included ideas such as making it safe to fail, don’t scapegoat someone around an idea that doesn’t work, but try a new path and move toward succeeding. Don’t setup people to fail, because that drags everybody down. Document things even when everybody supposedly knows those things. The list goes on, but that’s a good base for the ideas.

Check out [Dreamwith Studios](http://www.dreamwidth.org/) for more of Denise’s work.

For those like me, that either weren’t at OS Bridge or missed this session, this one will be put up live at some point so keep an eye out for the videos being posted. For an immediate fix, Joe has a podcast at [JavaScript Jabber](http://javascriptjabber.com/). He’s also got a site related to doing TDD & JavaScript at [Test Driven JS](http://www.testdrivenjs.com/).

**DIY Electric Vehicles**

My friend, beverage connoisseur and JavaScripting genius Jerry Sievert [@jerrysievert](https://twitter.com/jerrysievert) strolled by and mentioned DIY Electric Vehicles, DIY Electric Cars, DIY Electric Bikes and DIY DIY DIY DIY Stuffs. So I packed and headed to this workshop without any original plan to attend anything at this time.

This was a solid session with an introduction to electric vehicles, what they look like, how they work, what types of batteries are good for this use and coverage of Benjamin Kero’s [@bkero](https://twitter.com/bkero) DIY Electric Bike. Really cool stuff, and something that I really want to expand on and connect even more tech, similar to this plus something like [Helios Bars](http://ridehelios.com/).
