[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Software/Reviews/VM-Depot/i-jKHvLd9/0/O/hupstream-250.png)](http://www.hupstream.com/en/)Today [Microsoft released](http://blogs.msdn.com/b/interoperability/archive/2013/01/09/getting-started-with-vm-depot.aspx), with partnerships with a number of companies including [Basho](http://www.basho.com), [Hupstream](http://www.hupstream.com/en/) and [Bitnami](http://www.bitnami.com), the [VM Depot](http://vmdepot.msopentech.com/). I’ve always followed Bitnami, so it’s really cool to see their VM releases for [Jenkins (CI Build Server)](http://vmdepot.msopentech.com/Vhd/Show?vhdId=49&version=49), [WordPress](http://vmdepot.msopentech.com/Vhd/Show?vhdId=3&version=3), [Ruby 1.9.3 stack](http://vmdepot.msopentech.com/Vhd/Show?vhdId=76&version=77), [Node.js](http://vmdepot.msopentech.com/Vhd/Show?vhdId=37&version=37) and about everything you can imagine out their along side our [Basho Riak CentOS image](http://vmdepot.msopentech.com/Vhd/Show?vhdId=66&version=79). [![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Software/Reviews/VM-Depot/i-NKgR7Fs/0/O/bitnami-logo-698c72fe38321edf260c28da17b2d9e7.png)](http://www.bitnami.com)If you want a great way to get kick started with Riak and you’re setup with Windows Azure, now there is an even easier way to get rolling.

Over on the [Basho blog we’ve announced the MS Open Tech and Basho Collabortation](http://basho.com/blog/technical/2013/01/09/Riak-on-VM-Depot-from-MS-Open-Tech/). I won’t repeat what was stated there, but want to point out two important things:[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Software/Reviews/VM-Depot/i-Rf5pL8M/0/O/logo.png)](http://www.basho.com)

1. Once you get a Riak image going, remember there’s the whole community and the Basho team itself that is there to help you get things rolling via the [mail list](http://lists.basho.com/mailman/listinfo/riak-users_lists.basho.com). If you’re looking for answers, you’ll be able to get them there. Even if you get everything running smoothly, join in anyway and at least just lurk. 🙂
2. [The RTFM value factor is absolutely huge for Riak](http://docs.basho.com/). Basho has a superb [documentation site here](http://docs.basho.com/riak/latest/tutorials/installation/). So definitely, when jumping into or researching Riak as software you may want to build on, use for your distributed systems or the Riak Key Value Databases, check out the documentation. Super easy to find things, super easy to read, and really easy to get going with.

[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Software/Reviews/VM-Depot/i-hbzcLj6/0/O/VMDepotLogo.png)](http://vmdepot.msopentech.com/List/Index)

So give [Riak a try on Windows Azure via the VM Depot](http://vmdepot.msopentech.com/Vhd/Show?vhdId=66&version=79). It gets easier by the day, and gives you even more data storage options, distribution capabilities and high availability that is hard to imagine.

# New Relic & The Rise of the New Kingmakers

[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Software/Reviews/VM-Depot/i-KG2cCXR/0/O/KingmakersCover.jpg)](http://redmonk.com/sogrady/2013/01/09/the-new-kingmakers-the-book/)In other news, my good friends at New Relic have released a new book in partnership with [Redmonk](http://redmonk.com/) Analyst Stephen O’Grady @, have released a book he’s written titled [*The New Kingmakers, How Developers Conquered the World*](http://blog.newrelic.com/2013/01/09/the-rise-of-the-new-kingmakers/). You may know New Relic as the huge developer advocates that they are with the great analytics tools they provide. Either way, give a look see and read the book. It’s not a giant thousand page tomb, so it just takes a nice lunch break and you’ll get the pleasure of flipping the pages of the book Stephen has put together. You might have read the blog entry that started the whole [“Kingmakers” statement](http://redmonk.com/sogrady/2010/09/09/the-new-kingmakers/), if you haven’t, give [that a read](http://redmonk.com/sogrady/2010/09/09/the-new-kingmakers/) first.

I personally love the statement, and have used it a few times myself. In relation to the saying and the book, I’ll have a short review and more to say in the very near future. Until then…

Cheers, enjoy the read, the virtual images and happy hacking.
