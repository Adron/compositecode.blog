Whew, it’s been a total blast working at [Basho](http://basho.com/). I’ve accomplished a ton of things. Riak is a solid distributed database system and I’m glad to have worked with the team on advocating its use, teaching distributed systems ideas and concepts and generally spreading the knowledge. I’ve seen some truly great things that people are hacking together, setting up for projects and redesigning old systems to utilize newer, better, faster and more capable distributed systems concepts and ideas. Some of the things I’m happy to have contributed to in my time at Basho.

- [![](https://photos.adron.me/Software/Misc-Images/Just-Icons-and-Bits/i-vC8qtgK/0/O/tier3.gif)](http://www.tier3.com/)I helped negotiate and get an effort started that came to fruition with Tier 3 releasing a Riak CS backed object store for their customers. A very cool feature added to their already formidable enterprise cloud offerings. Read more about that here “[Tier 3 Object Storage](http://basho.com/tier-3-object-storage-powered-by-riak-cs/)” and here “[Tier 3 Launches Global Object Storage](http://www.tier3.com/press/releases/tier-3-launches-global-cloud-object-storage)“. Their implementation is really nice, including many geographic regions of accessibility, S3 API compatibility and high end storage capabilities that offer a bigger punch of performance than your average object storage in the cloud!
- I launched both the [Seattle Riak](http://www.meetup.com/Seattle-Riak/) (*up to 52 members now!*) and [Portland Riak](http://www.meetup.com/Portland-Riak/) (*up to 74 members now!!*) groups started, which you should join, they’re a good time, good conversation and great information.
- I partnered with Troy Howard **[@thoward37](https://twitter.com/thoward37)** to run the second year of Node PDX. Basho was excellent enough to contribute not just a few bucks but also sent Chris Meiklejohn **[@cmeik](https://twitter.com/cmeik)** out to speak at the conference.
- [![](https://photos.adron.me/Software/Misc-Images/Just-Icons-and-Bits/i-2H6RWZv/0/S/google-io-2013-logo-S.png)](https://developers.google.com/events/io/)I worked with the [Google team to get the awesome Tyler Hannan over to the Google I/O Conference onto the Google I/O Data Panel](http://basho.com/basho-is-speaking-at-google-io/). The panel was solid, thanks to Tyler [@tylerhannan](https://twitter.com/tylerhannan), [@juliaferraioli](https://twitter.com/juliaferraioli) and the Google Team for making this happen!
- I got to work directly with a number of people at [Windows Azure](http://www.windowsazure.com/en-us/), [AWS](http://aws.amazon.com/) and [EngineYard](https://www.engineyard.com/) in deploying Riak, testing out how the respective images ([azure VM Depot](http://vmdepot.msopentech.com/List/Index?sort=Featured&search=riak)  & [AWS AMI](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/B00AMRXCQA/)) and deployments ([Riak EDS](https://www.engineyard.com/partner/riak-eds)) would work. In the end, this has been a great opportunity to learn more about the latest and greatest of each of these services. I’ve been impressed as they’ve each been doing a seriously kick ass job lately!
- [![](https://photos.adron.me/Software/Misc-Images/Just-Icons-and-Bits/i-HhQQj9W/0/S/newrelic-logo-square-rgbhex2-S.jpg)](http://newrelic.com/)Had a blast working on the [New Relic Riak Plugin](http://newrelic.com/plugins/basho-technologies/93) with [Drew Kerrigan](https://github.com/drewkerrigan) for the recently released New Relic plugin ecosystem. Code is also available via the [Github Basho Riak Plugin repo](https://github.com/basho/basho_riak_plugin).
