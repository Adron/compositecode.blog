Hello everybody and especially the upcoming ML4ALL attendees! For those that haven’t registered, here’s the [link for that](https://ti.to/ml4all/ml4all-2018), and for those that have *welcome aboard*! Here’s two of the excellent speakers that will be presenting at ML4ALL Paige ([@DynamicWebPaige](https://twitter.com/DynamicWebPaige)) and Suz ([@Noopkat](https://twitter.com/noopkat))!

Paige & Suz both work on the amazing, now somewhat famous Microsoft Developer Advocacy team others like Peter Bright [(@drpizza](https://twitter.com/drpizza)) wrote about in “[Microsoft’s renewed embrace of developers developers developers](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2017/05/microsofts-renewed-embrace-of-developers-developers-developers-developers/)” and James Governor ([@monkchips](https://twitter.com/monkchips)) wrote in “[On Hiring Jesse Frazelle: Microsoft’s developer advocacy hot streak continues](http://redmonk.com/jgovernor/2017/09/06/on-hiring-jessie-frazelle-microsofts-developer-advocacy-hot-streak-continues/)” and I even [wrote a blurb about](/2017/09/30/the-developer-advocates-observations-on-microsofts-new-competence/) many months ago. They’ve added even more awesome to a great team and will be coming to [ML4ALL](http://ml4all.org/) to do more of that.

## Suz HintonNew Tools for Old Problems – Machine Learning for Access

Suz is going to show you how to level up around accessibility using script injection and machine learning  using some historic ‘guerilla accessibility’! She’ll give both a technical and social commentary of what the missed opportunities to use new technology for greater good. But alas, albeit we’ve missed those opportunities Suz has ideas on how we can code ourselves out of that difficult box!
