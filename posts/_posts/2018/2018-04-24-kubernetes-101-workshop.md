Today I TA’d (Teacher’s Assistant) a course with Bridget at [GOTO Chicago Conference](https://gotochgo.com/). There were a number of [workshops](https://gotochgo.com/2018/workshops) besides just the Kubernetes 101 like; [Working Effectively with Legacy Code](https://gotochgo.com/2018/workshops/101) with Michael Feathers ([@mfeathers](https://twitter.com/mfeathers)), [Estimates or NoEstimates](https://gotochgo.com/2018/workshops/80) with Woody Zuill ([@WoodyZuill](https://twitter.com/WoodyZuill)), [Testing Faster](https://gotochgo.com/2018/workshops/86) with Dan North ([@tastapod](https://twitter.com/tastapod)), [Data Science and Analytics for Developers (Machine Learning)](https://gotochgo.com/2018/workshops/81) with Phil Winder ([@DrPhilWinder](https://twitter.com/DrPhilWinder)), and so many others that I’d love to have multi-processed all at the same time! Digging through Kubernetes from a 101 course level was interesting, as I’ve never formally tried to educate myself about Kubernetes, just dove in. My own knowledge is very random about what I do or don’t know about, and a 101 course fills out some of the gaps for me.

The conference is located in a cool and sort of strange place for a conference, out kind of in the lake, called the [Navy Pier](https://navypier.org/). Honestly, I dig it, it’s a cool place for a conference. I enjoyed the ~15 minute walk from the hotel to the location too, because it’s right there on the tip of the pier, as shown in the fancy map below.

![chicagonavypier](/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/chicagonavypier1.jpg)

The workshop is going well. Bridget is filling up student’s brains and I’m going to dork around Kuberneting some Cassandra and Node.js for my talk. I’m pretty stoked as the talk has given me a good excuse to delve into some Node.js again, from a nodal systemic viewpoint, “[Node Systems for Node.js Services on Nodes of Systemic Nodal Systems](/confs-meetups-events/nodes-for-nodes-node-nodal-system/)” this Thursday.
