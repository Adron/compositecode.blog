I’ve been pondering getting into some after hours training, maybe some weekend training sessions, or something along those lines.  My main focus that I’d like to aim for, but I’m unsure of the demand, is training for [cloud computing](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_computing).  I would like to offer specific sessions on [Amazon Web Services](http://aws.amazon.com/) or [Azure](http://www.microsoft.com/windowsazure) and how to setup projects, use the various storage mechanisms, and good architecture practice.

**My questions are:**

1. Is there demand for this?
2. Is the demand for short 1-2 hr sessions, day long sessions, or even multi-day sessions that are all encompassing?
3. Is there a demand for advanced level (lots of hands on coding & architectural practices – ala suggested patterns and practices)?

Please leave a comment or two in regards to this, I’d love to get a feel for this possibility and would absolutely get something started (maybe for free even).
