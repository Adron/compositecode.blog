So here is the lowdown on what I will be presenting, demoing, and showing to the attendees & anyone who is interested at [Engage 2010](http://blogs.webtrends.com/neworleans10/).  If you are attending and have not signed up on the [Engage Crowdvine Site](http://neworleans10.crowdvine.com/), please do so.  There are some great conversations going on there.

One other thing I wanted to point out, is that you MUST attend the Ignite in New Orleans.  [Ignite](http://ignite.oreilly.com/2010/01/ignite-comes-to-new-orleans---february-1-2010.html) events are a blast!  Portland, Oregon regularly has some of the largest Ignite Events, and since we are bringing a whole lotta Portland love, and NOLA is well known for some great parties, I know the [NOLA Ignite](http://ignite.oreilly.com/2010/01/ignite-comes-to-new-orleans---february-1-2010.html) will be awesome!  So make sure to attend.

[![](https://blogs.webtrends.com/img/Engage-2010-Speaker-Badge.png)](http://blogs.webtrends.com/neworleans10/70140000000SleO "Official Webtrends Engage 2010 Speaker")

First off, Brian Gallagher and myself will be showing off some awesome technology for video tracking at the science fair break out sessions.  There will be a [Tuesday Science Fair](http://bit.ly/d0MplR) and a [Wednesday Science Fair](http://bit.ly/aHMdqg), and I will be there with Brian going over the tracking.  So if you miss us one day make sure to check it out the next day!

The next speaking session is the enterprise & business intelligence topic I have mentioned in some of my previous blog entries.  The session is titled [Make Analytics Work across your Enterprise](http://bit.ly/7bKQEm).  Our topic summarized is, [The nerd of the Enterprise;  ETL, EPM, ERP, BI, CRM, and how the alphabet soup works together for real Enterprise Business Intelligence and Insight](http://bit.ly/7bKQEm).

The final engagement I have is a topic near a dear to my work here at [Webtrends](http://www.webtrends.com/).  Over a year ago a rock star team of developers; [Rob D](http://www.linkedin.com/in/derstadt), [James K](http://www.linkedin.com/pub/james-king/9/514/857), and myself started working on the Data Exchange, REST Web Services for [Webtrends](http://www.webtrends.com/).  Of course now those have been in production and are now on v2.0 already!  Rob has gone on to rock the current [iPhone Webtrends Application](http://www.webtrends.com/Products/Analytics/iphone.aspx) (Check out the [Webtrends Blog Post Too](http://blogs.webtrends.com/blog/2010/01/25/video-walkthrough-of-the-webtrends-iphone-app/)) & James is coding away at some of the awesome infrastructure that keeps all the data flowing smoothly on the back end (to the web services, to the Insight UI, the whole thing ? James is awesome at that).  So we built these services many months ago and now I get to present them as a [workshop session at Engage](http://bit.ly/8cVsch).  Open web services based on good clean [REST Architecture Principles](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representational_State_Transfer) have always been an interest of mine and I have been and will continue to be stoked that we at [Webtrends](http://www.webtrends.com/) have these available for customers!  So be sure, if interested, to check out the [Workshop:  The RESTful Way ? How to Use Webtrends REST Services](http://bit.ly/8cVsch).

Also for further reading, check out some of my past entries on REST Services;

- [REST, So Many Nuances, The Self Signed SSL Cert Override](http://www.adronbhall.com/blogs/technology__software_development/post/2009/03/02/REST-So-Many-Nuances-The-Self-Signed-SSL-Cert-Override.aspx)
- [REST Web Services Synchronous Request](http://www.adronbhall.com/blogs/technology__software_development/post/2009/05/12/REST-Web-Services-Synchronous-Request.aspx) that points at the [Webtrends Developer Network Blog Entry](http://developer.webtrends.com/community/dx/blog/2009/05/08/rest-web-services-synchronous-request).
- [Just a Bit More REST](http://www.adronbhall.com/blogs/technology__software_development/post/2009/02/26/Just-A-Bit-More-REST.aspx) which has a really bad example of [unit tests](http://www.adronbhall.com/blogs/technology__software_development/post/2007/06/20/Software-Development-Testing-Tip-O-The-Day.aspx), which are more integration tests, running against some REST Web Services.
- [File Extension ".svc" Annoyance](http://www.adronbhall.com/blogs/technology__software_development/post/2009/02/11/File-Extension-svc-Annoyance!.aspx)
- [RESTful .NET by Jon Flanders](http://press.oreilly.com/pub/pr/2183) is probably one of the best books on the WCF based REST Services.  So if you are looking for a solid guide, this is a great place to get just that.
