Recently I have watched a couple of good videos on InfoQ regarding Agile Software Development.    Alistair Cockburn’s "[I Come to Bury Agile, Not to Praise It](http://www.infoq.com/presentations/cockburn-bury-not-praise-agile)", which ironically is a pro-Agile talk.  The other talk that I dug was Scott Ambler’s "[Agile by the Numbers:  What People Are Really Doing in Practice](http://www.infoq.com/presentations/ambler-agile-by-the-numbers)".

**[beware, random interspersed doses of sarcasm below]**

After watching these two talks, I have decided on a new path I am going to take.  I am going to start mistrusting and hating agile.  You know, so I can fit in and stuff.
