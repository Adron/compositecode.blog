This year’s ML4ALL is coming up real soon, more details over on the site, but here I’m providing some retrospective talks from last year to get you ready for this year’s ML4ALL!

In this talk Kirsten Westeinde ([@kmkwesteinde](https://twitter.com/kmkwesteinde)) provides us insights into how your customers can gain superpowers through effective data use. Last year, [I introduced Kirsten](/2018/05/04/now-kirsten-westeinde-rich-jolly/).

This year’s event is coming up, if you want to get deeper into, pr present on machine learning, learn ways to improve your use of, and eliminate biases like this then [ML4ALL](http://ml4all.org/) is going to be a great conference for you. Check it out, we have an open [CFP](http://ml4all.org/speak.html), [tickets](https://ti.to/ml4all/ml4all-2019) are available (early bird still!)
