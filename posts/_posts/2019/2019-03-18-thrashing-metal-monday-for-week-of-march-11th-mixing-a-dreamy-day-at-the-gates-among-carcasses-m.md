In an effort to throw you some wild curve balls, here’s some wild options for Monday. Again a brash way to awaken and take on the world!

First, this is a real curve ball, not exactly my cup of tea I’m looking for, but I’ve thrown some lemon into the cup and it’s actually a pretty sweet flavor of tunes. This band [Dream State](http://dreamstateofficial.com/) I’ve known about for some time and they keep sneaking back into my playlist. This is one of their newer tunes In This Hell.

This next carcass, they’ve been in the mix for a long while now for me. Ever since rolling upon the flanged wheel into New York City and my buddy Mike picking up one of their albums, their first album [Heartwork](https://youtu.be/ZpbpOgUybBM) to be specific, at random. We then spent the next 20 hours on the train riding back to NOLA listening to [Carcass](https://www.facebook.com/OfficialCarcass/) and our other finds from New York City.
