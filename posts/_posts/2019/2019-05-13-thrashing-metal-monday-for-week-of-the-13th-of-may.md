A new band I just learned about this last week is Bloody Hammers. Listen to those vocals, traditional dark prodding rhythms and melody. It’s eerie in the best of ways and provides that melancholy horror movie feel so well! Beautiful!

You can check out their [website](http://www.bloodyhammers.com/) which has good details and information, but their material is of course out there on [Bandcamp](https://bloodyhammers.bandcamp.com/) too so check that out and pick up some tunes!
