[Windows SDK Blog](http://blogs.msdn.com/windowssdk/)

The Windows SDK Blog is rather cool and recently [Karin Meier](mailto:karinm@microsoft.com) posted an entry with a whole BUNCH of [samples for .NET 3.0](http://blogs.msdn.com/windowssdk/archive/2006/11/13/looking-for-something-new-in-a-sample.aspx).  Specifically…

- 430 Windows Presentation Foundation samples
- 140 Windows Communication Foundation samples
- 60 Windows Workflow Foundation samples
- 200 New to Vista (Win32/COM-based) samples
- 23 Cross technology samples
