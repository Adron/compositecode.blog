While ramping back up for heavy duty OLAP & BI work there where a few names that kept popping up.  These are definitely the heavy hitters in the field.  For my (and you fellow reader’s) future reference.

- The first person is [Mosha Pasumansky](http://sqljunkies.com/WebLog/mosha/default.aspx).  He is the father of MDX at Microsoft.  His blog is full of great material on MDX and Cube info in general.
- Richard Tkachuk wrote an [Introduction to MDX Scripting in Microsoft SQL Server 2005](http://technet.microsoft.com/en-us/library/ms345116.aspx).
- One guru that does a lot of work in the field is [Brian Knight](http://www.whiteknighttechnology.com/cs/blogs/brian_knight/default.aspx), who I had the fortune of working with on a gig down in Jacksonville for a major ETL/BI/Cube Project.
- [Vidas Matelis BI Blog](http://www.ssas-info.com/VidasMatelisBlog/).  Digging, dug, data mining.
- For a mix of BI/OLAP/SSAS/SSIS/ASP.NET/TFS and more, check out [Richard Hundhausen’s Blog](http://sqlblog.com/blogs/rhundhausen/default.aspx).
- Another great blog is [SQL Server Code, Tips and Tricks, Performance Tuning](http://sqlservercode.blogspot.com/).  This blog has a good mix of material covering internals and interviews with people in regards to SQL Server and related topics.
- [Andy Leanord](http://sqlblog.com/blogs/andy_leonard/default.aspx) seems to be a good read too.  He has various [design patterns associated with SSIS Development](http://sqlblog.com/blogs/andy_leonard/archive/2007/10/14/ssis-design-pattern-read-a-dataset-from-variable-in-a-script-task.aspx).
- The [Microsoft PerformancePoint Team Blog](http://blogs.msdn.com/performancepoint/default.aspx) is also a great read.  Good stuff.  🙂
- Musings on [SQL Server 2005/SSIS/SSAS](http://sqljunkies.com/WebLog/macaw/default.aspx) is on topic for this particular blog roll.
- [Ward Pond’s SQL Server blog](http://blogs.technet.com/wardpond/archive/2007/08/16/pond-s-seventh-law-applied-again.aspx) is also a good read.
