I found the [solution Kevin Dente’s blog](http://weblogs.asp.net/kdente/archive/2007/08/09/visual-studio-install-asking-for-xp-sp2-on-vista-the-solution.aspx).  Hat tip!  If you run into the problem, the solution is really easy.

The key in HKEY\_LOCAL\_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Windows NT\CurrentVersion\AppCompatFlags\Layers called %tempdir%\setup.exe, DELETE IT.

All done.
