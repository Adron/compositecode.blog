Here I am sitting at work, typing away at some code, and I took a short break to quickly read a blog entry, “[Confessions of a Terrible Programmer](http://kickin-the-darkness.blogspot.com/2007/09/confessions-of-terrible-programmer.html)“.  After checking out these confessions of a terrible programmer I was reading through the comments.  While reading through them I stumbled upon one that I had to post, just merely a quote, but a great quote at that.

“*If I tell you I’m good, you would probably think I’m boasting. If I tell you I’m no good, you know I’m lying.*” – Bruce Lee

I must say, truly a great one.
