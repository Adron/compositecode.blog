About two weeks ago I put together an [initial list of JavaScript Tools & IDEs](/2011/07/22/javascript-development-environment/). Over the last week at OSCON, talking to a number of people who are into and using JavaScript on a daily basis, I’ve come up with a larger list of tools and IDEs.

***Browsers On Your Dev Machine***

- [Firefox](http://www.mozilla.com/)
- [Chrome](http://www.google.com/chrome)
- [Safari](http://www.apple.com/safari/)
- [Opera](http://www.opera.com/)
- [Internet Explorer](http://www.beautyoftheweb.com/)

***Tools I’m Using…***

- [Visual Studio 2010](http://www.microsoft.com/visualstudio/en-us/products/2010-editions) w/ [ReSharper 6](http://www.jetbrains.com/resharper/) (Microsoft + JetBrains)
- [RubyMine](http://www.jetbrains.com/ruby/) (Jetbrains)
- [WebStorm](http://www.jetbrains.com/webstorm/) (Jetbrains)
- [Firebug on Firefox](http://getfirebug.com/)
- [Node.js](http://nodejs.org/)

***Tools I’m Trying Out…***

- [Emacs](http://www.gnu.org/software/emacs/)
- [Vi/Vim](http://www.vim.org/)
- [JS Fiddle](http://jsfiddle.net/)
- [Jasmine](http://pivotal.github.com/jasmine/)
- [JS Test Driver](http://code.google.com/p/js-test-driver/)
- [Coffeescript](http://jashkenas.github.com/coffee-script/)
- [Chirpy](http://chirpy.codeplex.com/) (Coffeescript -> JS Compilation)
- [Web Workbench](http://visualstudiogallery.msdn.microsoft.com/2b96d16a-c986-4501-8f97-8008f9db141a) (Mindscape)
- [Aptana](http://www.aptana.com/)
- [Notepad2](http://www.flos-freeware.ch/notepad2.html)
- [Textmate](http://macromates.com/)
- [Sublime Text 2](http://www.sublimetext.com/2)

***Suggestions From The [Twitterverse](http://twitter.com)! Thanks Everybody.***

- [JoshuaPoehls](http://twitter.com/#!/JoshuaPoehls) (Joshua Poehls) JS Fiddle, Jasmine, JS Test Driver
- [davidalpert](http://twitter.com/#!/DavidAlpert) (David Alpert) Firebug + Aptana
- [xinmyname](http://twitter.com/#!/xinmyname) (Andy Sherwood) Chirpy, Web Workbench
- [encosia](http://twitter.com/#!/encosia) (Dave Ward) VS2010, PhpStorm, Notepad2, Vim, & Textmate depending on the project…
- [danemorgridge](http://twitter.com/#!/danemorgridge) (Dane Morgridge) Macvim, Textmate, and Chrome
- [lazycoder](http://twitter.com/#!/lazycoder) (Scott Koon) WebStorm, vim Chrome dev tools, Google Closure Compiler
- [jerrysievert](http://twitter.com/#!/jerrysievert) (Jerry Seivert) Textmate, jshint bundle which activates on save.
- [liammclennan](http://twitter.com/#!/liammclennan) (Liam Mclennan) Sublime Text 2, CoffeeScript and Shell Scripts
- [lucisferre](http://twitter.com/#!/lucisferre) (Chris Nicola) Vlm, js Vlm Plugins, & Jetbrain’s Stuff.
