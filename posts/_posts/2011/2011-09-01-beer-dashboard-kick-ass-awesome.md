A good friend of mine, [Eric Sterling](http://twitter.com/almost_eric), has put together the most ultimate Dashboard EVER! A [beer dashboard](http://www.baileystaproom.com/?p=1002) at [Bailey’s Taproom](http://www.baileystaproom.com/). It even has foursquare integration so you know who the mayor is!  Represent!

[![Bailey's Beer Dashboard](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/Transit/Collections/Just-Whatever/i-RxrMwrg/0/S/BeerDashboard-S.jpg "Bailey's Beer Dashboard")](http://adronhall.smugmug.com/Transit/Collections/Just-Whatever/18036487_V7343V#1457411280_RxrMwrg-O-LB)

*Bailey's Beer Dashboard*

He put this together using Silverlight and worked with Jeff & team at Bailey’s to get it rendering perfectly on a giant flat screen. So now when you go to Bailey’s, you can see with a mere glance the state of every beer on tap!

The Oregon Live even had an article by [John Foyston](http://connect.oregonlive.com/user/johnfoysten/index.html) posted “[Bailey’s New Electronic Beer Menu](http://blog.oregonlive.com/thebeerhere/2011/08/baileys_new_electronic_beer_me.html)” on the new “beer screen” (I prefer “beer dashboard”).
