The next [Portland Docker Meetup](http://www.meetup.com/Docker-Portland-OR/events/166924632/) is going to be at [New Relic](http://newrelic.com/) offices in big pink. It looks to be a solid line up of topics, which I’ll be kicking off with a Docker intro. There will also be a Q & A session, lightning talks and some topic coverage of [drone.io](https://drone.io/) (I’m definitely looking forward to hearing about this topic).

## Database Stuff that aint RDBMS in Olympia

Fast forward to April 10th and I’ll be boarding the flanged wheels to come and visit Olympia, Washington to speak at the [Olympia South Sound Developers User Group](http://www.meetup.com/ssdevelopers/). The topic will be on “***Database Stuff that aint RDBMS***“! I’ll have more on that presentation, the deck and any other things that come up related to this. I \*might\* even have a video of the presentation afterwards.
