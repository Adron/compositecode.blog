## Let’s Make pdxbridge.js Happen!

The [pdxbridge.js](https://github.com/Adron/pdxbridge.js) project is all about determining if a bridge in Portland is up or down. Right now there are  several bridges that matter, that are on this list;

- [Broadway Bridge](http://web.multco.us/bridges/broadway-bridge)
- [Steel Bridge](http://www.oregon.gov/Pages/index.aspx)
- [Burnside Bridge](http://web.multco.us/bridges/burnside-bridge)
- [Morrison Bridge](https://web.multco.us/bridges/morrison-bridge)
- [Hawthorne Bridge](http://web.multco.us/bridges/hawthorne-bridge)

If we add other information to track about the bridges we might add the other 3 that exist and the new bridge that is being built. however the five listed are the only bridges that have a raised and lowered state, and in one case the Steel Bridge has a lowered, partially raised and fully raised state. As shown on the [pdxbridge.js](https://github.com/Adron/pdxbridge.js) badge I threw together (shown above).

To get involved with [pdxbridge.js](https://github.com/Adron/pdxbridge.js) go [add your input on this issue](https://github.com/Adron/pdxbridge.js/issues/1) I started to discuss our first meet, plan and hack.
