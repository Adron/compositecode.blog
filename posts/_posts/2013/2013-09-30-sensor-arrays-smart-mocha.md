…about the secret language that machines talk…

Last week I began a new effort to get in and not just sit on the sidelines and watch what my favorite startups are doing but actually talk to, increase the visibility of and get to know what’s going on. The team at [Smart Mocha](https://smartmocha.com) was more than happy to sit down and talk with me for a few. Topics went from sensor arrays, to big data and a bit of design patterns around that. It was a great conversation, great team and only increased my interest in what Smart Mocha is working toward!

I put together a video of an interview with the core team of Surj Patel [@surj](https://twitter.com/surj), Jake Olsen [@jakeo](https://twitter.com/jakeo) and Scott Osborn [@steve918](https://twitter.com/steve918) (also the company page on the team here: <https://smartmocha.com/meet-our-team/>). Check it out below and also, feel free to subscribe to the Vimeo Channel were I regularly post tech videos and screencasts “**[Shredding Code](https://vimeo.com/channels/shreddingcode)**“. After you check out the video, dive in further at **[Smart Mocha](https://smartmocha.com)**.
