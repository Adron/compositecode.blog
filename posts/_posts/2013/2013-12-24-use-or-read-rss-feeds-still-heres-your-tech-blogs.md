I wanted to get it out there to the public. It all started with Hadi [@HHariri](http://twitter.com/HHariri) converting his blog from WordPress to Jekyll using Github Pages. I’ve wanted to get a solid list of top blogs. One’s that have great content, solid writing, technical and mixed topics of tech, leadership and related to technology blogs.
