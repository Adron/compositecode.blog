Another quickie tip o’ the day.  When the build is broken in TeamCity, a good habit to gain is to “Take Responsibility” for the broken build.  Whoever broke it, or more specifically whoever is fixing it, should click on “Take Responsibility” so that others know that it is being fixed.

[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/photos/310121326_T6ziK-M.png)](http://adronhall.smugmug.com/gallery/5132068_GjVZt/1/310121326_T6ziK)

This way no one ever has to go run around asking who is working on the build.

[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/photos/310121353_ieZNJ-O.png)](http://adronhall.smugmug.com/gallery/5132068_GjVZt/1/310121353_ieZNJ)

Then once the build succeeds, one can run around and celebrate like a mad hatter.

[![](/images/adronhall.smugmug.com/photos/310121364_JeNQd-O.png)](http://adronhall.smugmug.com/gallery/5132068_GjVZt/1/310121364_JeNQd)
