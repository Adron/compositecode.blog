For that “keeping up” category entry this week I’ve hit up a few web site pages that have current topics of concern.

The first I dug up was “[Creating a managed service factory](http://www.codeproject.com/useritems/managed_service_factory.asp)” by [Martin Hinshelwood](http://geekswithblogs.net/hinshelm/Default.aspx). Martin does a great job of laying out an interesting idea for managing multiple services via a factory.  Read it, it is interesting (I’m still processing and groking what is going on here).

While reading through some of Martin’s material I stumbled into an entry on “[hosted TFS pilot program](http://blogs.msdn.com/jimlamb/archive/2007/05/30/hosted-tfs-pilot-program.aspx)” by Jim Lamb.  With the problems I have had with personally hosted TFS servers, and my return to Subversion and other solutions, I’d love to have access to a reasonably priced TFS hosted solution.  As a matter of fact, it would RULE!

On another topic, while checking out the latest Code Project e-mail I received I discovered another code generator I had not seen before.  It is called [Smart Code](http://www.codeproject.com/useritems/SmartCode-Code_Generation.asp) and it is open source!  I dug a little into the article and discovered it is maintained by a company called [Kontac](http://www.kontac.net/).  The produce business rules management software and other tools.  Check out [Kontac](http://www.kontac.net/)‘s [website for Smart Code](http://www.kontac.net/SmartCode.aspx).

The last article I read through was a write up on the ole’ trusty Command Pattern titled “[Behavioral Patterns:  Writing Command Pattern with C#](http://www.codeproject.com/useritems/Command_Pattern.asp)” by Francesco Carata.

Most of these articles I found I received via one of the weekly e-mails sent out by [Code Project](http://www.codeproject.com/).  [Code Project](http://www.codeproject.com/) is an awesome site with some great articles and great content contributors.
