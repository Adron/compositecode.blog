A quick tip o’ the day.  I also posted this answer on [WikiAnswers](http://wiki.answers.com/) for the question, “[What is the cardinality of a data type eg integer?](http://wiki.answers.com/Q/What_is_the_cardinality_of_a_data_type_eg_integer)“

*“Cardinality is the total number of unique occurrences of an entity (e.g. person, organization, or transaction) that can be included in a relationship.*

*A URI/URL, e-mail, phone number should be unique, providing a high cardinality. This means that each of the entity values would be represented at least once in a relationship.*

*A lower cardinality value would be something like a bit, or boolean value. This value will only occur twice before becoming redundant in a relationship.”*

Also find a great definition and relationship description at [datamodel.org](http://www.datamodel.org/) for [cardinality](http://www.datamodel.org/DataModelCardinality.html) and at [WikiPedia](http://en.wikipedia.org) of course there is an entry on the [cardinality subject](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardinality_(SQL_statements)).
