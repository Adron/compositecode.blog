I was complaining recently about getting MDX a week or so ago.  I wanted MDX generated from my Cube in [BIDS](http://msdn2.microsoft.com/en-us/library/ms173767.aspx).  Well I found a kick butt tool that helps one do that.  Not only does it help with getting the MDX, but it also provides printer friendly dimension usage, dimension “health checks”, and other functionality.  It is definitely one of the “helpful” tools out there that should be in the toolbox of any BI/Cube Developer.

So you [BIDS](http://msdn2.microsoft.com/en-us/library/ms173767.aspx) users, go check out the [BIDS Helper](http://www.codeplex.com/bidshelper).

([BIDS](http://msdn2.microsoft.com/en-us/library/ms173767.aspx) == [Business Intelligence Development Studio](http://msdn2.microsoft.com/en-us/library/ms173767.aspx))
