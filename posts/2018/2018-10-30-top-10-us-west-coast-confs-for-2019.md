I’ve been putting together a list of conferences that I want to aim to attend this coming year. I made it, then thought, “somebody else could use this list probably” so here it is. If you think of any other specific conferences I ought to add and attend please leave a comment. Enjoy!

March 7-10 is [SCALE Southern California Linux Expo](http://www.socallinuxexpo.org/scale/16x) in Pasadena, California

March 25-28 is O’Reilly [Strata](https://conferences.oreilly.com/strata/strata-ca) in San Francisco, California

April 26-28 is [LinuxFest Northwest](https://linuxfestnorthwest.org/conferences/2019) in Bellingham, Washington

June 3-5 is [Monitorama](http://monitorama.com/) in Portland, Oregon

June 10-13 is O’Reilly [Velocity](https://conferences.oreilly.com/velocity/vl-ca) in San Jose, California

June 10-13 is O’Reilly [Software Architecture Conference SACON](https://conferences.oreilly.com/software-architecture/sa-ca) in San Jose, California

July 15-18 is O’Reilly [OSCON](https://conferences.oreilly.com/oscon/oscon-or) in Portland, Oregon

August 21-23 is the [Open Source Summit](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/events/open-source-summit-north-america-2019/) in San Diego, California

September 9-12 is the O’Reilly [Artificial Intelligence](https://conferences.oreilly.com/artificial-intelligence/ai-ca) in San Jose, California

November 18-21 [KubeCon 2019](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/events/kubecon-cloudnativecon-north-america-2019/) in San Diego, California

**Without Dates –**Conferences that are really great that don’t currently have a date just yet.

Polyglot Conf in Vancouver BC

[Seattle Code Camp](https://seattle.codecamp.us/)

Microsoft Build

GDG DevFest

What others should I add that are awesome Seattle or immediate surrounding area conferences?
