Over the last few weeks the I’ve been putting together multi-cloud conversations and material related to multi-cloud implementation, conversations, and operational situations that exist today. I took a quick look at some of my repos on Github and realized I’d put together a [multi-cloud Node.js](https://github.com/Adron/multi-cloud) sample app some time ago and should update it. I’ll get to that, hopefully, but I also stumbled into some tweets and other material I wanted to collect a few of them together.
