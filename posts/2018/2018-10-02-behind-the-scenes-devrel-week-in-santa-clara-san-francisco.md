The following are some lagniappe, a little extra, about the behind the scenes adventures I’m off on when I travel or am in between coding. Ya know, coding being life and all.  😉

The [first episode](https://youtu.be/oV_epAVIIcY) in this series I posted a while back on my gear I use to record the Twitch sessions and pretty much everything else. So subscribe on my [Youtube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCHSCXYCMOOS-VSUdRsn13GQ) and on [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/adronhall) – the shows are coming to Youtube, and now and again I’ll pre-watch one with my Twitch audience.
