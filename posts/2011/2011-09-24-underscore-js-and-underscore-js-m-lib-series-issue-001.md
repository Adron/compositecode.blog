Recently I wanted some string manipulations that just weren’t available with plain JavaScript. I went digging and came up with the Underscore.js Library. It was a little confusing at first, and the string library is an extension of Underscore.js. At first it sounded like there were two Underscore.js Libraries, but finally I got it figured out.

Get **Underscore.js** here:

- [Underscore.js Site](http://documentcloud.github.com/underscore/)
- [Download Underscore.js](https://github.com/documentcloud/underscore/zipball/master) or [Fork it on Github](https://github.com/documentcloud/underscore/).

Get the Underscore.js “**Underscore.string**” here:

- [Underscore.js’s Underscore.string Site](http://edtsech.github.com/underscore.string/)
- [Download Underscore.strings](https://github.com/edtsech/underscore.string/zipball/master) or [Fork it on Github](https://github.com/edtsech/underscore.string)

Cheers! \m/
