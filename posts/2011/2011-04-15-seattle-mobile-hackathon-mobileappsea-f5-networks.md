This Saturday is the [Seattle Mobile Hackathon](http://www.mobileapphackathon.com/).  I’ve been preparing my machine for some mobile hacking of my own.  The event looks like it will be a blast, hopefully you’re signed up already, because it’s sold out and down to the waiting list.  🙂

So far my machine is shaping up like this:

- [Ubuntu 10.10](http://www.ubuntu.com/)
- [Ruby on Rails 1.9.2](http://rubyonrails.org/)
- [RubyMine](http://www.jetbrains.com/ruby/)

I am however torn between the mobile framework/SDK/APIs I want to use.  Some of the options include:

- [PhoneGap](http://www.phonegap.com/)
- [appMobi{!}](http://www.appmobi.com/)
- [jQuery Mobile](http://jquerymobile.com/)
- [Sencha Touch](http://www.sencha.com/products/touch/)

Needless to say, lots of options.  I’m a big fan of options!  Any other suggestions for stacks or suggestions on the mobile frameworks I’d appreciate.
