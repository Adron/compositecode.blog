A few of the good blog entries I have had the pleasure of reading the last few weeks.

[Writing Great Unit Tests:  Best and Worst Practices](http://blog.codeville.net/2009/08/24/writing-great-unit-tests-best-and-worst-practises/) by [Steve Sanderson](http://blog.codeville.net/).  This entry is just an overview of unit testing, but really well written.  Also Steve placed a few nice charts in defining unit & integration tests.  The combination just made for a nice refresher, or for the test newbie, a great introduction.

[Gergely Orosz](http://gregdoesit.com/) has some articles on NHibernate versus Entity Framework performance;  [Thoughts on ranking ORM tools](http://gregdoesit.com/2009/08/thoughts-on-ranking-orm-tools/), [NHibernate vs Entity Framework: a performance test](http://gregdoesit.com/2009/08/nhibernate-vs-entity-framework-a-performance-test/), and [Nhibernate vs Entity Framework ? a revised performance comparison](http://gregdoesit.com/2009/08/nhibernate-vs-entity-framework-%e2%80%93-a-revised-performance-comparison/).  The interesting conclusion is that there is no good effective way to compare ORM platforms.  I suppose, it best to compare on the specific metrics you desire in an ORM platform, then choose.

The [REST for ASP.NET MVC SDK](http://blog.maartenballiauw.be/post/2009/08/19/REST-for-ASPNET-MVC-SDK.aspx) entry is a good read.  First off it states what it is and why you would want to use it, which would be helpful for many.  It caught my eye solely because I would have loved to have the functionality 10 months ago for a project, instead of manually developing all the bits.  At least it is out now and I will surely be using it as such.

Last but not least, there is the [Facebook & ASP.NET MVC](http://www.robertschultz.org/2009/08/17/facebook-asp-net-mvc/) entry.  This is a useful entry, being I have just jumped onto a project doing some Facebook Development this is a very helpful entry in getting up to speed.
