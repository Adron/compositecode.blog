Almost a year ago I had left [WebTrends](http://www.webtrends.com) to kick off a start up.  I had always promised I'd return to [WebTrends](http://www.webtrends.com) if they'd have me back.  With that context, I completed this start up kick off and then headed back to [WebTrends](http://www.webtrends.com).  I did this for a couple of reasons.  The main one was the people I get to work with.  [WebTrends](http://www.webtrends.com) without doubt has one of the best crews of people out of all the software companies in Portland.  The services department to the engineering group to the sales team are all a great team of people.  They handle their jobs well, push forward with learning, and strive to make [WebTrends](http://www.webtrends.com) a great place to work and make sure that the products and services we offer get better and better by the day.

Another one of the reasons that I returned to [WebTrends](http://www.webtrends.com) is because of the industry itself.  Analytics, specifically web analytics, are hot.  Everything from [Twitter](http://twitter.com) tracking, sales trending, site analysis, and market studies, web analytics is an awesome field to be in.  [WebTrends](http://www.webtrends.com) is one of the leaders in that field.

[WebTrends](http://www.webtrends.com) over the years has had varying development methodologies and this brings me to my third main point of why I returned.  [WebTrends](http://www.webtrends.com) is steadily getting more and more [Agile](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agile_software_development).  In the methodology sense.  Anyone that has read my blog in the past knows I"m a fairly hard core [agile](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agile_software_development) advocate (I"ve seen more devoted like [Arlo Belshee](http://www.linkedin.com/pub/0/13b/ab), but I"m an advocate none the less).  From the [iteration](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agile_software_development) based to the [kanban boards](http://www.infoq.com/articles/agile-kanban-boards) to the pair or [promiscuous pairing](http://odeo.com/episodes/23587550-Agile-2008-Arlo-Belshee-Naked-Planning-Promiscuous-Pairing-and-other-Unmentionables) to functional teams, the agile style of team & project work is awesome.  [WebTrends](http://www.webtrends.com) has started down this road heavily and that is my cup of tea.

I was asked recently by some fellow .NETter geeks, "what have you been working on at WebTrends?"  To which I've been happy to say, "Iz Zecret!"  or the most part, what I've been working on has been on the down low lately, however, I?ll be blogging a LOT more about it real soon.  I'll be providing examples here, and on a new site that isn't published yet, about how we'll be doing some awesome "*fill in the blank"* – so stay tuned for that.
