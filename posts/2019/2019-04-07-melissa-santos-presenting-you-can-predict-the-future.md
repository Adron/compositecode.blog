Introducing Melissa Santos > [@ansate](https://twitter.com/ansate) < presenting “YOU Can Predict the Future?”.

![melissa-santos](/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/melissa-santos.jpg)Melissa has been working with computers and data since 2000, in fields from security to marketing to geography. She has a PhD. in Applied Math and considers herself both a statistician and a data scientist. Currently she is a data analyst at Pingboard, helping understand the customers and how they use the product.

Let’s get started with forecasting! We’ll look at simple linear trends, polynomial fits, ARIMA models, and using Facebook’s Prophet library. The goal is to introduce you to forecasting and help you try modeling the future. We will also look at goodness-of-fit measures so we can compare our models.
