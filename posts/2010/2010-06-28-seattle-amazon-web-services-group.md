If you’re into cloud technologies and such, you should swing by the Seattle [AWS](http://aws.amazon.com) Group tomorrow after work.  Go [sign up](http://sawsugjune2010.eventbrite.com/) on the [eventbrite site](http://sawsugjune2010.eventbrite.com/).  If you do go, leave a comment and I’ll be sure to introduce myself.

Here’s a blurb from the [eventbrite](http://www.eventbrite.com) site…

> “[Our next meeting is on Tuesday, June 29th, 2010.](http://www.sawsug.com/events-1/next-meeting)
>
> Learn how Amazon Web Services can provide efficient scale of IT infrastructure capacity to quickly meet growing business needs.
>
> Join us to learn about the exciting world of Cloud Computing and AWS! If you are a seasoned expert or just starting out with AWS, come share discussions on your AWS experiences (good and the bad), security architectures, best practices, automation and general cloud computing ideas with other members.
>
> For more information, please visit <http://www.sawsug.com>”

For more information about the group check out the [Seattle AWS Groups website](http://www.sawsug.com/).
