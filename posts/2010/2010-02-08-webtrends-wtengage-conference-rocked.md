Week before Mardi Gras, the Saints rocked and won the Super Bowl, and [@webtrends](http://twitter.com/webtrends) threw an awesome [#wtengage](http://search.twitter.com/search?q=wtengage) Conference.  Props out to the long list of [Webtrends](http://www.webtrends.com/) People there;  [@DashLavine](http://twitter.com/DashLavine), [@caseycarey](http://twitter.com/caseycarey), [@webtrendspeter](http://twitter.com/webtrendspeter), [@justinogarrity](http://twitter.com/justinogarrity), [@sullybridgetb](http://twitter.com/SullyBridgetB), [@kaykas](http://twitter.com/kaykas), [@justinkistner](http://twitter.com/justinkistner), [@michelewarther](http://twitter.com/michelewarther), [@mrdiggles](http://twitter.com/mrdiggles), [@yodera](http://twitter.com/yodera), [@vkenkal](http://twitter.com/vkenkel), [@derekfine](http://twitter.com/derekfine), [@ekrobi](http://twitter.com/ekrobi), [@mccook](http://twitter.com/mccook), [@robinoula](http://twitter.com/robinoula), [@benfogarty](http://twitter.com/benfogarty), [@noexg](http://twitter.com/noexg), [@thomschoenborn](http://twitter.com/thomschoenborn), [@mediachick](http://twitter.com/mediaChick), and others!  The event was awesome, great sessions, great knowledge transfer, great food, just great ? period!

Also must say the meetings, meets, and introductions were great.  I’m glad to have met dozens and dozens of people and get those names and faces connected.  Finally caught up with; [@seanpower](http://twitter.com/seanpower), [@johnlovett](http://twitter.com/johnlovett), [@christineconley](http://twitter.com/christineconley), [@ed1chandler](http://twitter.com/ed1chandler), [@bullfrogmedia](http://twitter.com/bullfrogmedia), [@ebeane](http://twitter.com/ebeane), [@cgrantski](http://twitter.com/cgrantski), [@drcasio](http://twitter.com/drcasio), [@nadolski](http://twitter.com/nadolski), and many others.  Then there are others I met and caught up with and still got more catching up to do; [@aknecht](http://twitter.com/aknecht) and [@bosilytics](http://twitter.com/bosilytics).

Seriously, all of these individuals are rock stars in the analytics community.  The conversations, new ideas, thoughts, and general kicking around New Orleans was a great time.  If you are interested in the next Webtrends Engage (and you ought to be if you do anything with analytics), check out the 2011 Conference coming to San Francisco.
