**Proposed Title:** Immutable Infrastructure Deployment of Distributed Systems

**Talk Outline/Conclusion:** In this talk I’ll show about numerous ways to deploy a distributed systems, specifically Apacha Cassandra, with other peripheral commentary about deploying other distributed systems like Kubernetes, Zookeeper and related distributed systems too. Once I cover numerous methods of deployment for and maintenance of distributed systems I’ll cover an actual deployment following patterns, practices, and ideas behind immutable infrastructure to efficiently and effectively deploy and maintain these distributed systems.

**Intended Audience:** operations, devops practitioners, database administrators, programmers working to automate systems, and others.

**Technical Skills & Concepts Required:** Basic understanding of networking, systems management, and operating systems, specifically Linux and POSIX compliant systems.
